Privacy & Cookies

Who we are

Emmy Tattoo, a tattoo artist working from Sainthood Tattoo, 80 Princes Avenue, Hull HU5 3QJ. You can reach us on WhatsApp (+44 7405 979137) or on Instagram at @emmy.tattoo.

What this site collects

There is no account and no newsletter on this website. The booking form on /book does not send your enquiry anywhere on its own: it composes a message and hands it to WhatsApp, and you press send. A copy of what you typed — name, idea, placement, size, budget, availability, notes, any email or reference link you gave — is stored on our hosting provider's servers for 90 days as a backup in case the WhatsApp handover fails, then deleted automatically. Everything after that happens in WhatsApp or Instagram, where those companies' own privacy policies apply to the conversation.

Cookies and analytics

The site loads Google Analytics 4, which sets cookies to count visits and see which pages are read. It is configured for page-view measurement only — no advertising, no remarketing and no profiling — and we never see anything that identifies you personally.

If you would rather not be counted, block cookies for this site in your browser settings, or read the site in a private/incognito window.

Other things stored in your browser

  • emmy-intro, in session storage, remembers that the opening animation has already played in this tab.

That key is not sent anywhere; it stays on your device.

Third-party content

  • The studio map is a Google Maps embed that only loads when you press "Load map".
  • Reviews are fetched from our own Cloudflare Worker, which relays public Google reviews; reviewer profile photos are served by Google.
  • Fonts are served by Google Fonts.

These providers see your IP address when their content loads.

Hosting

The site is served as static files by our hosting provider, which keeps short-lived logs (IP address, user agent) to run and protect the service.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask what data we hold, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. Because this website itself stores nothing that identifies you, most requests will concern the WhatsApp or Instagram conversations you have had with us.

Website Terms

Using this site

You may browse and share links freely. Everything else — the photographs of tattoos, the designs shown in them, the text and the layout — belongs to Emmy Tattoo and may not be copied, reproduced or used to advertise anything else without written permission.

Tattoo photographs

Images are of real, completed work and are shared with the clients' permission. They are examples, not a promise that a given design can be reproduced identically on different skin.

No medical advice

The aftercare guidance on this site is general information from a tattoo artist, not medical advice. If a tattoo shows signs of infection, or you have a medical condition that affects healing, speak to a pharmacist or your GP.

Availability and accuracy

Prices, availability and opening hours change; nothing on this site is a binding offer. We try to keep it accurate but do not guarantee the site is always available or error-free.

Links out

WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Google Maps and Google Reviews are run by other companies under their own terms.
